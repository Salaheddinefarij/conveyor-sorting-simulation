How to demonstrate this project in 90 seconds
  1. Press Start. Explain the feed, inspect and sort sequence. Every third product has a simulated defect.
  2. Switch on Conveyor jam. The controller will latch a fault after the movement timeout.
  3. Clear the jam. Show that the machine stays stopped.
  4. Press Reset fault, then Start. Explain why restart needs a separate action.
  5. Press Stop, choose Manual, and hold Jog to demonstrate output control.
